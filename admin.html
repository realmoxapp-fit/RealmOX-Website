Tournament settings

Use reward items with rank, kind, category, name, description, optional image_url, and optional fulfillment_notes. Configure frame entries for ranks 1–5 and avatar entries for ranks 1–10. A category rc row at rank 1 sets top-3 RC; rank 11 sets RC for ranks 11 onward. Defaults: 2,500 and 100 RC.

Copy the URL into the matching prize’s image_url field above and save.

Tasks and task rewards

Use requirement_type total_workouts, best_streak, or level. Tasks can grant title_reward and/or badge_reward.
