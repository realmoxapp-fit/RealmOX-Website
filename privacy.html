Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. About RealmOX

RealmOX is a fitness and wellness application operated under the RealmOX name. It provides workout planning, fitness-profile setup, progress and activity tracking, nutrition and hydration features, games, leaderboards, achievements, titles, referrals, Premium subscriptions, account management, and support.

This Privacy Policy explains what information RealmOX processes, why it is processed, how it is protected, when it may be shared, how long it is retained, and how you can request deletion or raise a privacy concern.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, authentication identifiers and account security information. Passwords are not stored in plain text; RealmOX stores a protected password hash.
  • Authentication information: Firebase Authentication is used for email/password authentication, email verification, password reset and Google Sign-In.
  • Fitness and wellness information: age, height, weight, gender, goals, activity level, workout days, difficulty, equipment preferences, nutrition, hydration and exercise activity that you provide.
  • App activity: workout plans, completion, daily progress, calories, macronutrients, water intake, streaks, XP, levels, game activity, scores, tournaments, achievements, titles and leaderboard information.
  • Referral information: referral codes, referral relationships, qualification status, reward status and related timestamps.
  • Premium information: subscription product, purchase/entitlement identifiers and lifecycle information required to verify Premium.
  • Support information: bug reports, feedback, account support requests and technical context you submit.
  • Technical/security information: authentication/session information, security events, timestamps, request information and server logs reasonably necessary to operate and protect the service.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to create and secure accounts; verify email addresses; support password reset and recovery; provide fitness and wellness features; save progress; operate games, leaderboards, achievements and titles; administer referrals and promotional rewards; verify Premium subscriptions; provide support; prevent fraud, cheating and abuse; maintain security; troubleshoot technical problems; and maintain RealmOX.

RealmOX does not use your fitness or wellness information to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent a disease or medical condition.

4. Fitness and Wellness Information

RealmOX is a general fitness and wellness service. Information such as weight, height, age, activity level, goals, nutrition, hydration and exercise activity is used to provide fitness features and personalization.

RealmOX is not a medical service. Content and recommendations are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

The current build does not request Health Connect or body-sensor permissions and does not import health data from those platforms.

5. Games and Tournaments

RealmOX stores game scores and tournament results for statistics, leaderboards, achievements, titles and tournament functionality. The current tournament system is skill-based and does not require an entry fee, wager, cash deposit or cash prize. RealmOX does not permit users to wager money on game outcomes.

6. Referral Programme

Under the current programme, when an eligible referred user completes the qualifying Premium purchase and the transaction is verified, the referrer and referred user may each receive a fixed ₹10 promotional reward. Referral data is used to attribute referrals, verify eligibility, prevent duplicate or fraudulent rewards and maintain reward records. See the Referral Programme Rules.

7. Premium Subscriptions

Premium may be offered as a recurring digital subscription. For Google Play distribution, in-app digital subscriptions are intended to use Google Play Billing unless an applicable Google Play programme permits another method. Price, billing period, renewal terms, benefits and offers are shown before purchase.

8. Service Providers and Sharing

RealmOX does not sell personal or sensitive user data. RealmOX uses Firebase Authentication and Google Cloud/Firebase services for authentication, database and application infrastructure, and Google Cloud Run for backend hosting. Support bug reports and feedback may be sent to the RealmOX administrator through Telegram. Google Play processes subscription purchase information for purchases made through Google Play Billing.

Information may also be disclosed where required or permitted by applicable law, to enforce our Terms, prevent fraud or abuse, protect users or RealmOX, or protect legal rights.

9. Data Security

RealmOX uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards including password hashing, authenticated API access, access controls, Firebase Authentication and encrypted HTTPS connections for production traffic. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

10. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, support users, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain security, resolve disputes and comply with legal obligations.

When an account is deleted, RealmOX deletes account and associated application data that is subject to deletion. Limited information may be retained when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal, regulatory, accounting or dispute-resolution purposes.

11. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

You can access this policy and the Terms in the app and on this website. You can request account and associated-data deletion in the app or through the public Delete Account page. Where applicable under Indian data-protection law and other laws that apply to you, you may have rights relating to access, correction, erasure, consent withdrawal and grievance redressal. Use the Support page to initiate a privacy request. We may verify your identity before acting on a request.

12. Children

RealmOX is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, use the support/privacy request mechanism.

13. Indian Data Protection

RealmOX is designed to operate with applicable Indian privacy and data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules and commencement provisions. Where those laws provide rights, obligations, notices, consent requirements, security obligations or grievance mechanisms applicable to RealmOX, we will apply them as they become applicable to the service.

14. Changes

We may update this policy when features, data practices, service providers, security practices or legal requirements change. Material changes will be communicated or consented to where required by law.

15. Contact and Grievance Mechanism

For privacy questions, data requests, deletion requests, bug reports, feedback or other support matters, use the RealmOX Support page or the corresponding mechanisms in the app.
