Account Control

Delete your RealmOX account

This page lets you request permanent deletion of your RealmOX account and associated application data without reinstalling the app.

This cannot be undone. Account deletion removes your RealmOX account and associated application data that is subject to deletion. Limited information may be retained where legally or operationally necessary, as described in the Privacy Policy.

Before you continue

  • If you have an active Google Play subscription, cancel it through Google Play first so you can manage future renewals.
  • You must authenticate with your current RealmOX password.
  • Google-only accounts must first have a RealmOX password set through the app's account-setup/password flow.
  • Never enter your Google password here unless it is also the RealmOX password you created for RealmOX. RealmOX does not ask for your Google password.

Confirm deletion

Your password is sent only over HTTPS to the RealmOX backend for authentication and is not stored by this website. The access token returned after login is kept only in memory for the immediate deletion request.
