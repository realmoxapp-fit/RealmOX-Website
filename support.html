Support

Help & Support

Use the form below for a privacy question, account issue, deletion question, bug or general feedback. The public website is also available if you cannot open the app.

In-app support

Signed-in users can use Help & Support to submit a bug report or feedback. Support notifications are routed to the RealmOX administrator through the configured support channel.

Privacy & deletion

For account deletion, use the dedicated Delete Account page. For other privacy requests, mention “Privacy Request” in your message.

Premium

For Google Play subscriptions, use Google Play subscription management to review or cancel your subscription.

Contact & privacy requests

Use the in-app Help & Support

Signed-in users can submit bug reports and feedback directly from RealmOX. These reports are stored with the account and may be forwarded to the RealmOX administrator through the configured support channel.

For a privacy request, clearly state that your message is a privacy request and describe what you need. Do not send passwords, authentication tokens, payment-card numbers or other secrets.
