Referral Information

RealmOX Referral Programme

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Current behavior: When a referred user signs up using a valid code and later obtains an active Premium entitlement verified by RealmOX, both users see a ₹10 reward message. The current referral flow does not pay cash or credit RC. Any future cash payment requires separate published terms before it is enabled.

1. Who can participate

Participation is available to eligible RealmOX account holders who comply with the app's Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

2. Qualifying event

A referral is marked complete when a new user signs up with a valid referral code and RealmOX verifies that the account has an active Premium entitlement.

3. Message only

After verification, the app displays a ₹10 reward message to both the referrer and referred user. This message is informational only; the current referral flow does not transfer money or credit RealmOX Currency. RealmOX will publish separate rules before enabling any cash payment.

4. One referral per referred account

Each referred account can qualify for the completion message only once. A referrer may receive messages for multiple eligible users, subject to these rules and anti-abuse controls.

5. Prohibited activity

  • Self-referral.
  • Creating or controlling duplicate/fraudulent accounts to obtain completion messages.
  • Manipulating purchases, subscriptions, referral codes or API requests.
  • Using bots, automation or other technical means to manufacture referrals.
  • Transferring or selling referral codes unless RealmOX expressly allows it.

6. Verification and timing

A referral may remain pending until RealmOX verifies the account and Premium entitlement. RealmOX may delay the completion message while carrying out reasonable fraud or transaction checks.

7. Reversals

If the qualifying entitlement is revoked, reversed, fraudulent, invalid, or otherwise fails these rules, RealmOX may remove the completed status or associated message.

8. Relationship to Premium

The referral status is associated with a genuine Premium entitlement. No part of the referral programme involves wagering or paying a fee to enter a chance-based reward scheme.

9. No gambling or chance

This programme does not involve wagering, staking, betting, lotteries, random drawings, or chance-based reward selection.

10. RealmOX Currency

The current referral flow does not issue RC. RealmOX Currency is an in-app currency used only for the RealmOX features expressly made available in the app.

11. Changes

RealmOX may update these rules for legal, platform-policy, fraud-prevention, operational or programme reasons. Material changes will be communicated where required.
