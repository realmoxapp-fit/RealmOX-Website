Official Programme Rules

RealmOX Currency Referral Programme

Last updated: 16 September 2026

Fixed reward: When an eligible referred user completes the qualifying Premium condition and RealmOX verifies it, the referrer and referred user each receive 2,000 RC.

1. Who can participate

Participation is available to eligible RealmOX account holders who comply with the app's Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

2. Qualifying event

A referral becomes eligible for reward only when the referred user successfully obtains an active Premium entitlement through an eligible Google Play subscription or an eligible RealmOX Currency redemption and the backend verifies the entitlement.

3. Reward

The reward is a fixed 2,000 RC promotional reward for the referrer and a fixed 2,000 RC promotional reward for the referred user. The reward amount does not depend on chance, game performance, leaderboard position, or random selection.

4. One referral per referred account

Each referred account can qualify for the referral programme only once. A referrer may receive rewards for multiple eligible users, subject to the programme rules and anti-abuse controls.

5. Prohibited activity

  • Self-referral.
  • Creating or controlling duplicate/fraudulent accounts to obtain rewards.
  • Manipulating purchases, subscriptions, referral codes or API requests.
  • Using bots, automation or other technical means to manufacture referrals.
  • Transferring, selling or trading referral codes or rewards unless RealmOX expressly allows it.

6. Verification and timing

Rewards are issued only after the backend verifies the qualifying Premium entitlement. A referral may remain pending while the entitlement is being verified. RealmOX may delay a reward when additional fraud or transaction checks are reasonably necessary.

7. Refunds and reversals

If the qualifying entitlement is revoked, reversed, fraudulent, invalid, or otherwise fails the programme rules, the associated referral reward may be withheld or reversed. RealmOX may also reverse rewards obtained through abuse.

8. Relationship to Premium

The referral reward is a supplementary promotional benefit associated with a genuine Premium entitlement. No part of the referral programme involves wagering or paying a fee to enter a chance-based reward scheme.

9. No gambling or chance

This programme does not involve wagering, staking, betting, lotteries, random drawings, or chance-based reward selection.

10. RealmOX Currency

Referral rewards are issued as RC, an in-app currency. RC is an in-app currency used only for the RealmOX features expressly made available in the app.

11. Changes

RealmOX may update these rules for legal, platform-policy, fraud-prevention, operational or programme reasons. Material changes will be communicated where required.
