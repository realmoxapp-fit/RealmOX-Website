Official Programme Rules

₹10 Referral Programme

Last updated: 8 September 2026

Fixed reward: When an eligible referred user successfully completes the qualifying Premium purchase and RealmOX verifies the transaction, the referrer and referred user are each eligible for ₹10.

1. Who can participate

Participation is available to eligible RealmOX account holders who comply with the app's Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

2. Qualifying event

A referral becomes eligible for reward only when the referred user successfully obtains Premium through an eligible transaction and RealmOX verifies the corresponding Premium entitlement.

3. Reward

The reward is a fixed ₹10 promotional reward for the referrer and a fixed ₹10 promotional reward for the referred user. The reward amount does not depend on chance, game performance, leaderboard position, or random selection.

4. One referral per referred account

Each referred account can qualify for the referral programme only once. A referrer may receive rewards for multiple eligible users, subject to the programme rules and anti-abuse controls.

5. Prohibited activity

  • Self-referral.
  • Creating or controlling duplicate/fraudulent accounts to obtain rewards.
  • Manipulating purchases, subscriptions, referral codes or API requests.
  • Using bots, automation or other technical means to manufacture referrals.
  • Transferring, selling or trading referral codes or rewards unless RealmOX expressly allows it.

6. Verification and timing

Rewards are issued only after the backend verifies the qualifying Premium entitlement. A referral may remain pending while a transaction is being verified. RealmOX may delay a reward when additional fraud or transaction checks are reasonably necessary.

7. Refunds and reversals

If the qualifying Premium transaction is refunded, revoked, charged back, fraudulent, invalid, or otherwise reversed, the associated referral reward may be withheld or reversed. RealmOX may also reverse rewards obtained through abuse.

8. Relationship to Premium

The referral reward is a supplementary promotional benefit associated with a genuine Premium transaction. The Premium transaction is for the Premium service itself; no portion of the Premium price is a wager or fee to enter the referral programme.

9. No gambling or chance

This programme does not involve wagering, staking, betting, lotteries, random drawings, or chance-based reward selection.

10. Future redemption

If RealmOX introduces a cash-out, redemption or other payment facility for referral rewards, additional rules may apply, including eligibility, minimum thresholds, identity verification, payment-method requirements, taxes, and applicable law. Such functionality will not be assumed to exist unless it is expressly made available in the app.

11. Changes

RealmOX may update these rules for legal, platform-policy, fraud-prevention, operational or programme reasons. Material changes will be communicated where required.
