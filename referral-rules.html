Referral Information

RealmOX Referral Programme

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Current reward: A new user enters a valid referral code during signup. After RealmOX verifies a qualifying paid Premium purchase, both users receive 100 RC. A ₹10 cash reward is planned but remains disabled; no cash is currently paid.

1. Who can participate

Participation is available to eligible RealmOX account holders who comply with the app's Terms & Conditions and applicable law.

2. Qualifying event

A referral is marked complete when a new user signs up with a valid referral code and later completes a paid Premium purchase verified by RealmOX. Promotional or administrator-granted Premium does not qualify. A qualifying purchase may be a verified Google Play subscription or Premium redeemed with RC.

3. Current reward

After verification, 100 RC is credited to the referrer and 100 RC is credited to the referred user. RC is an in-app currency and cannot be converted to cash. A ₹10 cash reward per user is planned for a future programme stage, but is disabled and is not currently paid or credited. Separate programme rules and a payout process will be published before cash rewards are enabled.

4. One referral per referred account

Each referred account can receive the 100 RC referral reward only once. A referrer may receive rewards for multiple eligible users, subject to these rules and anti-abuse controls.

5. Prohibited activity

  • Self-referral.
  • Creating or controlling duplicate/fraudulent accounts to obtain completion messages.
  • Manipulating purchases, subscriptions, referral codes or API requests.
  • Using bots, automation or other technical means to manufacture referrals.
  • Transferring or selling referral codes unless RealmOX expressly allows it.

6. Verification and timing

A referral may remain pending until RealmOX verifies the account and Premium entitlement. RealmOX may delay the completion message while carrying out reasonable fraud or transaction checks.

7. Reversals

If the qualifying entitlement is revoked, reversed, fraudulent, invalid, or otherwise fails these rules, RealmOX may remove the completed status or reverse the associated RC reward where technically and legally permitted.

8. Relationship to Premium

The referral status is associated with a genuine Premium entitlement. No part of the referral programme involves wagering or paying a fee to enter a chance-based reward scheme.

9. No gambling or chance

This programme does not involve wagering, staking, betting, lotteries, random drawings, or chance-based reward selection.

10. RealmOX Currency

Referral RC is credited to each user's RealmOX wallet and may be used only for the RealmOX features expressly made available in the app. RC has no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or transferred for money.

11. Changes

RealmOX may update these rules for legal, platform-policy, fraud-prevention, operational or programme reasons. Material changes will be communicated where required.
